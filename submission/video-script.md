# Video script: OnHours demo, version 2

Length 2:18.9, 1920 by 1080, narrated by Murf (voice Natalie, style Narration), with burned-in captions and a separate caption file. No music. Every picture of the product is the real app, recorded by script; the group chat, the home screen and the three cards are drawn for the film and use made-up names.

The words below are what is spoken. The captions show the same words with the name written OnHours; the voice is given "On Hours" so it says the name as two words. Times are from the finished narration track.

## Scene 1: The problem (0:00 to 0:14)

On screen: A drawn group chat on a phone. A forwarded picture of a printed notice arrives, then three neighbours ask which group they are in. The view slowly closes in on the notice. Over the last line the real app opens on the phone.

| 0:01.5 | 0:04.7 | Every few days, a photo like this lands in the street group chat. |
| 0:05.1 | 0:09.5 | Tomorrow's power cuts. And everyone does the same sum in their head. |
| 0:09.9 | 0:13.4 | When is my area off? And what can I still get done? |

## Scene 2: The product, end to end (0:14 to 0:42)

On screen: The phone, filmed from the running app. A tap on Photograph the notice, then the sample photo: a reading state, then six areas. Riverside East is chosen, a job is typed in plain words (charge the radio 30 min), Plan my day is pressed, and the day chart draws in. Beside the phone, a panel first says what the photo gives, then shows the AI-reads, code-decides flow.

| From | To | Spoken |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:13.9 | 0:19.8 | This is OnHours. Take a photo of the notice, and it reads every area, with its power cuts. |
| 0:20.3 | 0:25.5 | Choose yours. Keep your jobs, or type new ones in plain words. Then plan the day. |
| 0:26.5 | 0:33.0 | Every job gets one unbroken stretch when the power is on. Nothing starts, and then loses power halfway through. |
| 0:33.5 | 0:41.5 | The AI only reads the notice. Plain, tested code places every job, so the same notice always gives the same plan. |

## Checks on the narration

Each line was spoken by Murf as its own take and listened to by Whisper in two sizes (small and medium). The takes in the finished film have a word error of 0.12 or less in both against the script; the only differences are the name (heard as "on hours" or "on ours") and "plan first", sometimes heard as "planned first". The film's loudness is -16.1 LUFS with a true peak of -1.4 dBFS (ffmpeg ebur128). The commands are in BUILD-VIDEO.md.
